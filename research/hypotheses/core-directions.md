# Areas of research

1. **False-positive filtering.** What types of false heat detections are found in the available sources and by what signs can they be distinguished from fires?
2. **Thermal Memory.** Does the history of thermal observations of an area help to estimate a new discovery and for what period it should be taken into account?
3. **Spatial-temporal incident clustering.** What distances and time windows allow detections of a single event to be combined without merging nearby fires?
4. **Evidence Fusion / confidence.** How to reconcile the evidence of different satellites, weather and context in a clear assessment of reliability?
5. **Priority Engine.** What factors should determine the order of response and how to test the usefulness of the obtained ranking?
6. **Impact Corridor.** How to assess the potential direction and impact zone, taking into account wind, terrain, vegetation and objects of risk?
7. **Burn scar mapping.** How accurately does Sentinel-2 allow you to distinguish the boundaries of the burns and assess the severity of damage in different conditions?
8. **Sentinel-1 fallback.** Can a radar image supplement cloud analysis and what are the limitations of this approach?
9. **External datasets.** What open sets of weather, population, roads, infrastructure, forests, protected areas and historic fires are available, relevant and permitted to use?
10. **Evaluation methodology.** What independent data, time and territory partitions and metrics are needed to assess detections, clustering, confidence and priority?
